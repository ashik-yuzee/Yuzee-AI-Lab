04. How Each Course Is Built

Deakin (S306) Structure

  • Compulsory Core: 13–16 core units covering programming (SIT102), data science fundamentals (SIT112), discrete math (SIT192), computer networks, and algorithms.
  • Majors / Sequences: Data Science, Robotics, or Internet of Things (IoT) (6 credit points).
  • Electives: 4–6 elective units allowing cross-faculty study or extended software engineering minors.
  • Capstone / WIL: Professional practice and industry capstone team project (SIT374 / SIT306) or elective industry placement.
  • Delivery: Melbourne Burwood campus or flexible online / multi-trimester study.

Monash (C2001) Structure

  • Compulsory Core: Rigorous common core including Python/Java programming (FIT1045, FIT1008), Discrete Math (MAT1830), Continuous Math (MAT1841), Algorithms & Data Structures (FIT2004), and Object-Oriented Design (FIT2099).
  • Specialisations: Advanced Computer Science (including FIT3155 Advanced Data Structures) OR Data Science.
  • Electives: 8 free electives across university or faculty offerings (Deep Learning FIT3181, AI FIT3080).
  • Capstone / WIL: Two-semester capstone studio project (FIT3161/FIT3162) OR competitive Industry-Based Learning (IBL - FIT3045, 18 pts).
  • Delivery: Clayton campus (primarily on-campus cohort study, standard semester model).

Deakin focuses on applied discrete mathematics and practical statistical scripts.


Robotics vs Algorithmic Software: Deakin offers distinct undergraduate major sequences in Robotics and Internet of Things (IoT), providing direct lab access to physical automation devices, sensor networks, and microcontrollers. Monash approaches automation from a pure software, advanced algorithmic, and data modeling perspective within its standard Computer Science stream.


Curriculum Execution: Monash will make you write highly optimized code from scratch to pass algorithmic benchmarking units. Deakin will guide you faster toward deploying functional full-stack and machine-learning prototypes integrated with existing frameworks.
